# Sponge Cakes
## Prerequisites 
- 2 [9 in. / 22.86 cm] Cake Pans
- 2 Large Bowls
- Hand Mixer
- Cake Strips (optional)

## Ingredients
- 8 large eggs
- [1/4 tsp / 1.5 g] Salt
- [1 1/2 cups / 300 g] Granulated Sugar
- [1/4 cup / 60 ml / 57 g] Unsalted Butter
- [1 1/2 cups / 180 g] All-Purpose Flour (Sifted)
- [1/2 cup / 50 g] Cocoa Powder (Sifted)
- [3 tsp / 14 g] Baking Powder

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to [325°F / 165°C], grease cake pans, prepare cake strips if using, and lightly melt the butter.
2. In the first bowl, sift the flour, cocoa powder, and baking powder together.
3. In the second bowl, add eggs and salt, and begin to whip the eggs. Slowly add the sugar to this bowl as you whip, whipping until the mixture forms a ribbon that doesn't disappear for about 3 seconds when lifting your whisk. Optimal timing on this is about 15 minutes of whisking.
4. Fold the flour mixture into the eggs until no large streaks are visible.
5. Incorporate the melted butter, being careful not to overmix.
6. Evenly distribute the batter into each pan. There should be a little over [800 g / 28 oz] of batter total.
7. Bake for 25 minutes, and begin to check if it is done (toothpick or fork in the middle coming out clean) in 5-minute intervals.
8. Completely cool each cake for at least 5 minutes, score the top of the cake being used as the bottom layer, and then chill until ready to decorate.

# Hazelnut Icing
## Prerequisites 
- Stand Mixer
- Rubber Spatula

## Ingredients
- [1/2 cup / 113 g] Unsalted Butter at Room Temperature
- [1 1/4 cups / 300 g] Hazelnut Butter or Hazelnut Spread
- [1 1/2 cups / 180 g] Powdered Sugar (Icing Sugar)
- [1/4 cup / 60 ml] Heavy Whipping Cream

## Instructions
1. Blend butter and hazelnut butter or hazelnut spread in stand mixer on a low speed using a paddle attachment.
2. While maintaining low speed, slowly add your powdered sugar.
3. Turn up the speed to medium to beat out any lumps, scraping the bowl with a rubber spatula to ensure everything is mixed.
4. Turn the mixer back down to low speed, and add cream in a steady stream.
5. Finish by reverting the mixer to a medium speed for at least one more minute, or until beaten smooth.

# Decoration
Spread icing on the first layer of the cake (the scored one), stack the second layer on top, then ice around the cake as desired. For garnish, top the cake with crushed Ferrero Rocher Dark Chocolate Hazelnut Chocolate Bar.
